Questions

Before you book

Do you take walk ins?

Yes. About half of our tables are kept for walk ins every evening. Booking guarantees your time.

Is there a vegetarian or vegan option?

Five pizzas are vegetarian. The marinara is vegan as served.

Can I order for delivery?

This demo has no delivery. A real version would link to the delivery partner here.

Do you have gluten free dough?

Not yet. Our oven shares surfaces with wheat flour, so we cannot promise a safe meal for coeliac guests.

What if I need to cancel?

Cancel up to two hours before your time at no cost. There is no deposit.

Do you host groups?

Yes, up to 14 guests. Use the form and add a note, and we reply within the day.
